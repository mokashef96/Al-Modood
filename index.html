Madd (Lengthening) المد
2 to 6 Beats
Rule / Cause
Natural or Secondary Cause
Standard Duration
2 to 6 Beats
Madd literally means prolongation or expansion. In Tajweed, it refers to prolonging the voice with a vowel sound when meeting specific letters.
Quranic Example
Nūḥīhā (We reveal it to you (contains all 3 Madd letters))
